# 🚀 CareerPath AI – Career Roadmap & Skill Gap Finder

An AI-powered career planning application that helps students identify their skill gaps, assess their career readiness, and build a personalized learning roadmap to achieve their dream job.

## 📌 Overview

Choosing a career is easy, but knowing what skills to learn, where to start, and how to prepare for a specific role can be challenging.

**CareerPath AI** solves this problem by analyzing a student's current skills, target career, experience level, and available study time using AI. It generates a personalized learning plan to help students progress toward their career goals.

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Career Analysis** – Personalized analysis based on your target job and existing skills.
- 📊 **Career Readiness Score** – Estimates your current preparation level.
- 🎯 **Skill Gap Analysis** – Identifies your strengths, weak skills, and skills you need to develop.
- 📅 **8-Week Learning Roadmap** – A structured weekly learning plan.
- 💡 **Portfolio Project Suggestions** – Project ideas to strengthen your resume.
- 📚 **Learning Recommendations** – Suggestions for learning resources and technologies.
- 📈 **Progress Tracking** – Track your roadmap completion.
- 🔄 **Regenerate Roadmap** – Generate a new career plan.
- 📥 **Downloadable Roadmap** – Save your personalized learning plan.
- 📱 **Responsive Interface** – Designed for desktop and mobile devices.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Enter your current technical skills.
2. Select or enter your dream job.
3. Provide your education and experience level.
4. Choose how many hours you can study per week.
5. Select your preferred learning style.
6. Generate your personalized career analysis using AI.
7. Explore your skill gaps and 8-week roadmap.
8. Track your progress and work toward your target career.
